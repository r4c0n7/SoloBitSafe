Setup guide

From a bare LilyGo T-Display-S3 to your first signed payment. Take your time with step 4: the recovery words are the only backup of your coins.

The two buttons

BTN1 (BOOT, next to USB-C)
short: display on/off · twice: rotate · hold 2 s: Bluetooth window for 120 s · in a dialog: confirm
BTN2 (IO14, on the other side of USB-C)
short: next page · hold 5 s: reset Wi-Fi settings · in a dialog: reject
Both
hold both 10 s, then BTN1 long: delete the wallet (only on the device)

  1. What you need

    • LilyGo T-Display-S3 with 16 MB flash and 8 MB PSRAM
    • a USB-C data cable
    • pen and paper for 12 or 24 recovery words
    • the SoloBitSafe app on Android, Windows or Linux (optional – the browser works too)

  2. Install the firmware

    • Open the Flash page (flash.html) in Chrome or Edge, plug in the board and choose Full install.
    • Later updates go the same way with Update – wallet, settings and Wi-Fi stay.
    • After the first start the display shows the SoloBitSafe start screen.

  3. Connect to Wi-Fi

    • The device opens the access point SoloBitSafe-Setup (password YourOwnCoins).
    • Connect your phone, choose your Wi-Fi in the portal and enter its password.
    • The device restarts and shows its address, for example 192.168.1.50. Holding BTN2 for 5 s starts the portal again.

  4. Create or restore the wallet

    • Open https://<device address>/wallet, compare the certificate fingerprint with the one on the display, then choose a passphrase (at least 6 characters).
    • New wallet: the words appear only on the device display, four at a time. Write them on paper, never photograph them, never type them into a computer.
    • Restore: enter your words on the device itself – BTN1 short: next letter, BTN1 long: previous, BTN2: take.

  5. Pair the app

    • USB: plug the device in and compare the fingerprint in the app with the display.
    • Wi-Fi: the app finds the device on the network; the display asks "PAIR APP?" – confirm with BTN1.
    • Bluetooth: hold BTN1 for 2 s, then enter the six-digit passkey shown on the display into the app.

  6. Receive

    • The main address bc1q… is shown in the app, in the browser and on the display, also while locked.
    • Before sharing it, use "Check on device" and compare it with the display.

  7. Send

    • The app builds the transaction; the device shows the receipt with the full recipient address, amount and fee.
    • Check everything on the display, then press BTN1 to sign or BTN2 to reject. Change always returns to the main address.

  8. Use other wallets

    • Sparrow, Electrum and HWI detect the device over USB as a Jade-compatible hardware wallet. Every signature still needs BTN1.

  9. Stay safe

    • Keep the recovery words offline and in a safe place – with them you can restore the wallet on a new device.
    • Install updates only from SoloBitSafe: the device accepts only signed firmware and never an older version.
    • If the device is lost, restore your words on a new device and move the coins.

The firmware and app repositories are private for now. Source of this website
