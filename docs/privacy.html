Privacy

SoloBitSafe collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking – not on this website, not in the app and not on the device. Below is every place where data leaves your hands, and to whom.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the pages (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). The site sets no cookies.

Fonts and images are served from this site. The 3D model and the web flasher load their scripts from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), which sees your IP address.

The web flasher talks to your board only after you pick it in the browser dialog. Firmware images come from this site; nothing is uploaded.

The app

Everything stays on your phone or computer: watch-only descriptors, labels, contacts and settings. The seed never reaches the app.

To show balances and history, the app asks the Electrum or Esplora servers you choose (by default public servers such as electrum.blockstream.info or mempool.space). These servers see your addresses and your IP address. You can use your own server or Tor.

Android permissions: Bluetooth / nearby devices to talk to the board, location only because older Android versions require it for Bluetooth scanning, USB for the cable connection, fingerprint for the optional app lock. None of this is sent to us.

The device

The seed is stored encrypted on the board and never leaves it. Settings and contacts stay on the board.

The board connects to your Wi-Fi, to the mining pool you set (which sees your payout address), to mempool.space for block height, balance and fees, and to CoinGecko for the exchange rate.

Its web pages load nothing from third parties; fonts fall back to the ones on your computer.

Donations and contact

Donations are Bitcoin transactions and therefore public on the blockchain. Questions and reports go through the GitHub repository of this website.
